We only collect information necessary to provide and improve our services, manage our digital product catalogs, and publish visual content. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties.

2. Pinterest API Integration & Authentication

Our application uses official Pinterest API v5 integration solely to manage and publish pins and product collections for Worlds Scrapbook. Authentication is processed securely via Pinterest OAuth 2.0. We do not store or share your Pinterest credentials or private user data.

We implement standard security measures to protect API tokens and configuration settings. User authorization can be revoked at any time directly through your Pinterest Account Settings.
